## 📌 Project Overview

This activity explores the purpose, features, and practical applications of **Google Meet** and **Google Forms** as essential digital tools for communication, collaboration, learning, and information management.

Through this activity, our group learned how to navigate both platforms, understand their important functions, and apply their features through practical demonstrations and an interactive quiz.

These platforms demonstrate how technology can make communication more accessible, simplify information gathering, support learning assessments, and improve productivity in academic and professional settings.

---

## 🎥 Google Meet

**Google Meet** is an online video-conferencing platform that allows users to communicate and collaborate virtually.

It can be used for:

- Online classes
- Virtual meetings
- Group discussions
- Presentations
- Interviews
- Consultations

Some of its important features include:

- Camera and microphone controls
- Screen sharing
- In-meeting chat
- Live captions
- Meeting links
- Participant management
- Background effects
- Meeting recording for supported accounts

Google Meet allows students, teachers, professionals, and healthcare workers to connect even when they are in different locations.

---

## 📝 Google Forms

**Google Forms** is a digital tool used to create, distribute, and manage online forms.

It can be used to create:

- Surveys
- Quizzes
- Registration forms
- Feedback forms
- Assessments
- Data-collection forms

Google Forms allows users to collect responses in real time. It also automatically organizes the results using charts, summaries, and individual response records.

The collected responses can be connected to **Google Sheets**, making the information easier to arrange, review, and analyze.

## 🩺 Application to Nursing

Google Meet and Google Forms can also be useful in nursing education and healthcare settings.

**Google Meet** may be used for:

- Online health education
- Virtual meetings
- Nursing conferences
- Group consultations
- Professional training
- Collaboration among healthcare workers

**Google Forms** may be used for:

- Patient feedback
- Health surveys
- Learning assessments
- Attendance monitoring
- Registration forms
- Collection of non-confidential information

These tools can help nursing students and healthcare professionals communicate, collaborate, and organize information more efficiently.
