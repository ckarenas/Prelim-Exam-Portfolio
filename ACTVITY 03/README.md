Activity 03 — Social Media Infographic

Take Care of Your Mental Health

Created by: Carlson Kerk Arenas
Program: Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN)
University: Ateneo de Davao University



📌 Project Snapshot

Project Type: Social Media Infographic
Theme: Mental Health Awareness and Stress Management
Target Audience: Teenagers and students
Main Message: Understanding stress and caring for mental health can support a healthier and more balanced daily life.

“Take Care of Your Mental Health.”

This infographic was created to share important information about mental health in a clear, organized, and visually engaging format.

Instead of using long paragraphs, I divided the topic into short sections that help readers understand what mental health is, why it matters, what may cause stress, how stress can appear, and what actions may provide support.

💡 The Idea Behind the Infographic

The idea for this project came from the challenges that students may experience while managing schoolwork, relationships, responsibilities, and personal changes.

Stress can affect a person's mood, concentration, interests, and daily activities. Because of this, I wanted the infographic to encourage students to recognize changes in themselves and others and to understand that asking for support is important.

The design communicates its message through simple explanations, familiar examples, and supportive reminders that can be read quickly on a social media platform.

🧠 What Mental Health Is

The first section introduces mental health as the way a person thinks, feels, and reacts each day.

It also explains that mental well-being can affect different parts of a student's life, including:

School performance

Friendships

Hobbies and interests

Emotions and reactions

Everyday decision-making

This opening section gives readers a simple foundation before presenting the other information.

❗ Why It Matters

The infographic emphasizes that mental health challenges can make school and daily life more difficult.

It highlights how taking care of the mind may help a person:

Focus more effectively

Feel emotionally better

Manage daily responsibilities

Participate more fully in school and personal activities

This section establishes the importance of paying attention to mental well-being instead of ignoring signs of stress.

📚 Common Stressors

The infographic identifies several situations that may contribute to stress among students:

Homework overload and deadlines

Feeling out of control or pressured

Conflicts with friends or family

Lack of sleep or too much screen time

Major life changes, such as moving, examinations, or changes in one's social life

Including realistic examples makes the message more relatable to the intended audience.

🔎 Signs You Might Be Stressed

Another section helps readers recognize possible signs of stress.

These signs include:

Feeling tired, anxious, or moody

Having trouble focusing or making decisions

Losing interest in activities that are usually enjoyable

Wanting to avoid school or social events

This section encourages greater self-awareness and reminds readers to notice changes in their thoughts, feelings, and behavior.

🌱 Tips to Stay Well

The final information section presents five brief and memorable reminders:

01 — Recognize Changes

Pay attention to noticeable changes in mood, behavior, interests, and daily routines.

02 — Engage With Empathy

Respond to another person's experiences with patience, kindness, and understanding.

03 — Ask and Listen

Create space for honest conversation and listen carefully without immediately judging or dismissing the person's feelings.

04 — Connect to Support

Reach out to a trusted person or appropriate support resource when additional help is needed.

05 — Help if Urgent

Take serious or urgent concerns seriously and seek immediate assistance from a responsible adult, qualified professional, or emergency service.

Together, these reminders show that mental health support can begin with awareness, empathy, communication, and connection.

🎨 Design Direction

For the visual direction, I wanted the infographic to feel calm, supportive, organized, and approachable.

I used rounded information panels, clear section labels, illustrations, and generous spacing to separate the content into manageable parts. This makes the long vertical infographic easier to follow from top to bottom.

The overall style is friendly enough for a student audience while still treating the topic with care and seriousness.

🌿 Color Choices

The design uses a limited and consistent color palette.

Dark Teal

Dark teal is used for the main title and section headings. It provides strong contrast and creates a calm, dependable appearance.

Sage Green

Sage green is used for the information panels. Its connection to nature, balance, and calmness supports the mental wellness theme.

White and Off-White

The light background and white text create breathing space and improve readability throughout the design.

Orange and Light Blue Accents

Orange and light blue appear in the illustrations. These accents add warmth, variety, and visual interest without distracting from the information.

🔤 Typography

The typography combines a bold display typeface with a clean and readable body font.

Display Typography

The large title and section headings immediately attract attention and establish a clear visual hierarchy.

Body Typography

The supporting text uses a simpler typeface so that the information remains easy to read, even in a social media format.

The contrast between the two typography styles gives the infographic both personality and clarity.

🖼️ Illustration and Layout

The illustrations support the message by showing situations connected to student life and mental well-being.

These visuals include:

Students reading, listening to music, and playing a keyboard

A student experiencing stress while working at a desk

A calm figure surrounded by growing plants and flowers

The illustrations make the topic feel more relatable and help separate the infographic's major sections. Rounded panels and centered labels create a consistent structure, while the vertical arrangement guides the reader through the information in a logical order.

🎯 Purpose of the Project

The purpose of this activity was to practice transforming a health-related topic into a clear and engaging social media infographic.

Through this project, I was able to apply:

Information organization

Visual hierarchy

Color consistency

Typography selection

Illustration placement

Concise health communication

Audience-centered design

🌱 Learning Reflection

This activity taught me that effective infographic design requires more than adding attractive visuals. The information must also be accurate, carefully organized, easy to understand, and appropriate for its intended audience.

As a nursing student, I also learned the importance of communicating health information with clarity and empathy. When health statistics are included, they should be supported by a reliable source so that readers can trust the information being presented.

The final output reflects my effort to combine mental health awareness with a calm, student-friendly visual design.
